## Key Findings

1. **Accuracy improves monotonically with gain**: from 0.5206 (gain=0) to 0.6621 (gain=10)
2. **Forgetting peaks around gain=2.5** (0.0542) then slightly decreases
3. **Variance increases substantially at high gains**: std goes from 0.0172 (gain=0) to 0.1510 (gain=10)
4. **Diminishing returns**: accuracy gain from 0→3 is +0.0864, but from 3→10 is only +0.0551
5. **The "knee" is at gain=3.0**: good accuracy-variance tradeoff before diminishing returns

## Recommendation

**Recommended gain value: 3.0**

Rationale:
- Accuracy: 0.6070 ± 0.0810 (matches ablation study's VISÃO-full at 0.607)
- Forgetting: 0.0535 ± 0.0473 (manageable)
- BWT: -0.0669
- Located at the knee of the accuracy curve before diminishing returns
- 3.5x lower variance than gain=10.0
- Proven consistency with the ablation study results

### Alternative recommendations:
- **For max accuracy** (accepting high variance): gain=10.0 (accuracy=0.6621, std=0.1510)
- **For conservative/safe choice**: gain=2.0 (accuracy=0.5958, std=0.0660)
- **Pareto-optimal set**: [0.0, 0.5, 10.0]

## Context from Ablation Study
